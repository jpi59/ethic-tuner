Ethic Tuner is a local chromatic tuner for Android.

Features:
• Chromatic tuning and instrument presets for strings, piano, woodwinds and saxophones.
• English or Latin note names.
• A4 calibration from 424 to 456 Hz.
• Light and dark themes with portrait and landscape layouts.

Privacy:
• Audio is analysed in memory and discarded.
• No audio files, accounts, analytics, advertising or network permission.
• Preferences remain on the device.

The app is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later.

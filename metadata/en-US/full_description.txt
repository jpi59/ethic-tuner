Ethic Tuner is a local chromatic tuner for Android.

Features:
• Chromatic tuning and instrument presets for strings, piano, recorder, woodwinds and saxophones.
• English or Latin note names and A4 calibration from 424 to 456 Hz.
• A clear in-tune state with a centred green inverse needle; the last stable reading remains visible if the input stops.
• Optional local beep and haptic confirmation when a stable note enters the tuning window.
• Optional Hz reference showing the measured frequency and equal-temperament target for the selected A4 value.
• Device-provided noise reduction when available, with an in-app switch.
• Light and dark themes that adapt to portrait and landscape layouts.

Privacy:
• Microphone samples are analysed in memory and discarded.
• No audio files, accounts, analytics, advertising or network permission.
• Preferences remain on the device; Android backup is disabled.

Ethic Tuner is a tuning aid. Readings can vary with microphone quality and environmental noise.

The app is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later.
